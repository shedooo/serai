1. `musig_key` allows identity point as a participant key. While this is mathematically handled, it could be a vector if an application assumes all participant keys have a discrete logarithm known to someone (the identity point logarithm is known to be 0). If the caller doesn't prevent this, someone could trivially claim the identity point as their key.
